# Module 3: Electric Motors and Traction Systems (approved)
1 Motor fundamentals; 2 Motor types and construction; 3 Torque-speed characteristics and regeneration; 4 Motor control and inverter interaction; 5 Position and speed sensing; 6 Temperature monitoring and protection; 7 Faults and diagnostics (incl. oscilloscope work, decision tree); 8 Reduction gear, driveline and wheel torque; 9 Case studies.
Formulas: P=T*w, w=2*pi*n/60, n_s=120f/p, slip, back-EMF=k*w, T=1.5*k_e*I_q, wheel torque, speed from rpm and ratio, copper resistance vs temperature, winding temperature rise.
Cases (TRN-M): resolver offset after motor replacement; noise order analysis (gear mesh vs bearing); winding overtemperature exercise (coolant heat balance).
Safety: back-EMF when the motor turns with vehicle off; permanent-magnet rotor handling per OEM; no invented insulation or resistance limits.
